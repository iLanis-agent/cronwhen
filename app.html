CronWhen

Paste a cron expression. See it in plain English and the next times it runs.
Rules from the crontab(5) manual page (man7.org). Fields: minute 0-59, hour 0-23, day of month 1-31, month 1-12 or names, day of week 0-7 (0 and 7 are Sunday) or names. Lists (1,15), ranges (1-5), steps (*/15, 0-30/10) and the nicknames @yearly, @annually, @monthly, @weekly, @daily, @hourly are supported. If both day-of-month and day-of-week are restricted (neither contains a *), the job runs when either matches: "30 4 1,15 * 5" runs on the 1st and 15th plus every Friday. Times are computed in UTC; a real cron runs in its server's time zone, and daylight saving shifts can skip or repeat runs, which this does not model. Some cron flavours differ (Quartz has seconds and a year, Kubernetes and GitHub Actions have their own notes, and some add L, W, # or ?): this follows the Vixie-style crontab. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
