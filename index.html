CronWhen
30 4 1,15 * 5 does not mean what most people think.
Open the app
30 4 1,15 * 5 runs at 04:30 on the 1st and 15th and every Friday, because when both day fields are set, either one matches.

What it does

  • Plain-English reading of any 5-field expression or nickname
  • The next runs, in UTC and your own time zone
  • The day-of-month plus day-of-week OR rule, spelled out
  • Clear errors for out-of-range or malformed fields
Follows the Vixie-style crontab(5) rules. Times in UTC. Other cron flavours differ. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
